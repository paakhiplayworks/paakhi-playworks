How Next Up works

Every level shows a sequence of numbers that follows a hidden rule. Work out the rule, then enter the next number. For example:

2, 4, 8, 16, ?

Each number is multiplied by 2, so the answer is 32. Later levels use arithmetic sequences, geometric sequences, square number patterns, alternating patterns and puzzles that mix mathematical operations.
